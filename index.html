How the score works

Each level is scored on efficiency, not on finishing: level_score = min(1.15, (par / your_actions)^2). Squaring is what makes it bite. Take twice par and you score 0.25, not 0.5. The game score is the average of the five level scores weighted by level number, so level 5 counts five times as much as level 1. Finish four of five and you are capped at 66.7 percent no matter how well you played.

That is the ARC-AGI-3 scoring rule, applied here. One difference, and it matters: ARC's baseline is the median of about 500 human players. Nobody has played these games yet, so par here is optimal play, computed by breadth-first search over the full state space. Optimal par is a harder yardstick than a human median. Beating 1.0 means you matched a solver that can see every move at once.
